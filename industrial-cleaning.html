Industrial Cleaning

Industrial Cleaning - Safe, Compliant, and Thorough

Our industrial cleaning teams specialize in complex, hazardous, and time-sensitive environments—helping facilities maintain safety, compliance, and operational reliability.

We provide comprehensive industrial cleaning services designed to maintain safe, compliant, and efficient work environments. Our trained team uses industry-grade equipment and approved cleaning methods to handle factories, warehouses, plants, and industrial sites with precision and care. We focus on safety, hygiene, and minimal disruption to operations.

Our Capabilities

Surface Preparation
  • High-Pressure Washing (Up to 4000 PSI): Removes dirt, grime, and loose paint.
  • SSPC-1 Hand Cleaning: Rust and corrosion removal for maximum paint adhesion.
  • Sandblasting: Efficient rust and coating removal for tanks, towers, and metal buildings.
Protective Coatings
  • Industrial Primers: Diamond Vogel V-Tech 600 for superior bonding.
  • Epoxy & Urethane Topcoats: High durability, chemical resistance, and UV protection.
  • Specialized Coatings: For ammonia, propane, and chemical storage tanks.
Access & Equipment
  • Zig Lifts & Ladders: Safe reach for tall towers and tanks.
  • Custom Spray Systems: Efficient and uniform coating application.
  • Portable Water & Fuel Systems: Self-sufficient in remote or rural job sites.
Safety & Certifications
  • OSHA-compliant safety programs and site-specific plans.
  • Fully bonded, licensed, and insured.
